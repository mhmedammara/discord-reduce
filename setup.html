Set up the thread link

For the person who runs the server. This makes one link; anyone who opens it can post their shrunk videos straight into your thread.

  1. In Discord (on a computer): Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook.
  2. Choose the channel your thread is in, then Copy Webhook URL.
  3. Paste it below, along with the link to your thread (long-press or right-click the thread → Copy Link).

Thread label: . Tell your friends the link should end with this when they connect, so they can tell it's yours.

Treat this link like a password. Anyone who has it can post to the thread (and nothing else). If it leaks, delete the webhook in Discord and make a new link here.

Nothing you paste here is stored or sent anywhere except to Discord when you press the test button.
